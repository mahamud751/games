The board game from every childhood afternoon. Flick the striker, sink your coins, and cover the Queen!

Board
You play white, your rival plays black. Pocket a coin of your colour to shoot again.
Sink the red Queen, then pocket one of your coins right after to cover it (+3).
If the striker falls in a pocket, that's a foul: one of your coins goes back to the centre.
★Pocket all 9 of your coins with the Queen covered to win the board.
