      Controls

      • Mouse / ←→ move the paddle
      • Click / Space launch ball · fire lasers
      • P / Esc pause
      • M sound on/off · F full screen

      Tip: where the ball hits the paddle decides its angle. Edges send it sideways. Break bricks in a row without touching the paddle to build a score multiplier.

      Power-ups

        Bricks

        • Colored: 1 hit
        • Steel: 3 hits
        • Explosive: blows up its neighbours
        • Gold: unbreakable
