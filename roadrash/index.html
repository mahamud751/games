HOW TO PLAY

  • ↑ / W Throttle
  • ↓ / S Brake
  • ←→ / AD Steer
  • Z / J Attack left
  • X / K Attack right
  • Space / Shift Nitro (N bikes)
  • P / Esc Pause
  • Knock a rider off while they carry a club or chain and it's yours.
  • Each knockout pays a bonus. Knocking out a cop pays more.
  • Crash with a cop nearby and you're busted, which costs you a fine.
  • Every crash damages the bike. At 0% it's wrecked and the race is over.
  • Running off the road slows you down. Hitting trees, rocks or signs knocks you off.

1ST PLACE

    Fight dirty

    Pull up beside a rival and swing with Z or X. Drain their health bar to knock them off, collect the KO bonus and take their weapon.

    Five highways

    Sunset Coast, Redwood Valley, Desert Canyon, Neon Downtown and Alpine Pass. Each has its own curves, hills, traffic and scenery.

    Build your ride

    Spend your winnings on seven bikes, from the rusty Sparrow 250 up to the Goliath 1300 N. Nitro bikes give you speed bursts on demand.

    Inspired by the 1991 classic Road Rash. Every graphic, track and sound here is drawn and synthesized in code.
